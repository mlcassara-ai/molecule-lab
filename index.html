Molecule Lab

Build molecules, steal and share electrons, and see what heat really does. Works on iPhone, iPad and computers.

Games

Electron Tug-of-War
Push sodium and chlorine together. Chlorine steals an electron, then + and − snap into table salt. Melt it with a torch, zap it back with electricity.
NaCl · stealing (ionic)
Share-a-Pair Water
Bring two hydrogens to an oxygen and watch them share pairs of electrons. Then boil it, and heat it way past boiling until the bonds snap.
H₂O · sharing (covalent)
Sugar Factory
Snap 24 atoms into glucose. Dissolve it in water, burn it black with a torch, or burn it for energy like your body does.
C₆H₁₂O₆ · 24 atoms
